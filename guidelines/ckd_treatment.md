# CKD treatment and referral (my summary, source: KDIGO 2024 CKD guideline)
Medicine for CKD with type 2 diabetes: an SGLT2 inhibitor is recommended when eGFR is 20 or higher.
Blood pressure medicine in CKD: an ACE inhibitor or ARB is recommended for people with CKD who have high blood pressure and increased albuminuria.
Monitoring known CKD: adults with CKD should have UACR and eGFR checked at least once a year to monitor progression.
When to refer to a kidney specialist: refer to a nephrologist when eGFR is below 30, UACR is 300 mg/g or higher, or kidney function is falling quickly.
